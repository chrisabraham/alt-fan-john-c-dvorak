# alt.fan.john-c-dvorak: charter site

The permanent home of the charter, rationale, and FAQ for
**alt.fan.john-c-dvorak**, a proposed unmoderated Usenet newsgroup for
discussion of John C. Dvorak (1946–2026), his columns, books, broadcasts,
and the No Agenda podcast.

- Live site: https://afjcd.org/
- Proposal published November 11, 2026; discussion in `alt.config`;
  a `newgroup` control message follows the discussion period.
- The group is a reader and listener initiative. It is not endorsed by
  the Dvorak family, his estate, or the No Agenda show.

Plain static HTML, no build step. `index.html` is the whole site.

Proposed and maintained by Chris Abraham · cja@well.com
